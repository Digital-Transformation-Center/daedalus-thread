title: Project Overview
description: Executive summary, charter, and ecosystem alignment for the Daedalus Thread open-source sUAS project.
---

Daedalus Thread addresses multi-year acquisition cycles for tactical Small Unmanned Aerial Systems (sUAS) by establishing an integrated parametric digital thread. The platform connects high-level system requirements directly to automated aerodynamic sizing, parametric CAD geometry, rapid physical fabrication, and flight test validation.

**Project Purpose**

Rather than delivering just a physical aircraft, Daedalus Thread delivers the entire digital thread that enables unique mission requirements to automatically turn into manufacturable, flight-ready designs. It serves as:

* **A Flagship Digital Transformation Demo:** Demonstrating how modern model-based systems engineering (MBSE) eliminates engineering silos and speeds up acquisition workflows.
* **A Digital Case Study (DCS) Testbed:** Identifying software interoperability bottlenecks, data-loss gaps, and digital friction across commercial and open-source toolchains.
* **An Open Collaboration Platform:** Releasing 100% of non-proprietary models, middleware, flight configurations, and guides to academic, industry, and defense partners.
* **A Workforce Development Asset:** Providing hands-on digital engineering experience for students, university interns, and early-career engineers.

---

**Core Objectives**

| Objective | Target Goal | Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Traceability** | 100% bi-directional traceability | Mission requirements flow directly down to component CAD parameters and Bill of Materials. |
| **Design Latency** | < 4 hours total | High-level mission parameter changes dynamically adjust 3D geometry and structural outputs. |
| **Rapid Fabrication** | < 48 hours turnaround | MBSE design lock to complete fabricated wing assembly ready for bench testing. |
| **Engineering Efficiency** | > 60% NRE reduction | Benchmark reduction in non-recurring engineering design hours compared to legacy workflows. |

---

**Ecosystem**

* **DTC Model Shop:** Serves as the physical prototyping engine, utilizing rapid FDM 3D printing, carbon fiber integration, metrology validation, and electronic bench testing.
* **Digital Enterprise Collaboratory (DEC):** Provides desktop computation and software environments to continuously analyze engineering tools and deliver actionable tool feedback.
* **Systems Integration Laboratory (SIL):** Offers a Modular Open Systems Approach (MOSA) low-cost testbed for integrating third-party sensors, edge AI compute, and autonomous flight controls.
* **DAF Digital Transformation Office (DTO):** Delivers unclassified, empirical proof of agile procurement and parametric digital thread execution.
