title: Digital Case Studies & Toolchain Research
description: Analysis of commercial digital engineering tools, software interoperability bottlenecks, and toolchain benchmarking.
---

Daedalus Thread serves as an empirical testbed for evaluating commercial and open-source digital engineering software. Through a series of Digital Case Studies (DCS), the project captures real-world data on software interoperability, toolchain friction, and workflow efficiencies to inform defense procurement and industry adoption.

**Research Framework & Alignment**

* **HAVOC Framework Evaluation:** Systematically scouts, tests, and benchmarks emerging commercial engineering tools on behalf of the DAF Digital Transformation Office (DTO).
* **Identifying Digital Friction:** Captures data-loss bottlenecks, format exchange friction, and handoff gaps across SysML, CAD, CAM, and physics solvers.
* **Standardized Data Exchange:** Evaluates open and standardized data exchange formats across the entire software stack to evaluate friction in data transfer.
* **Public Knowledge Base:** Publishes 100% of non-proprietary models, software middleware, and findings for ingestion by academic, industry, and small-business partners.

---

**Benchmarked Performance Targets**

| Metric Area | Target Benchmark | Operational Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **NRE Design Hours** | > 60% reduction | Quantifies time savings compared to standard acquisition and engineering workflows. |
| **Requirements Traceability** | 100% bi-directional traceability | Connects high-level operational requirements directly down to component CAD parameters and BOM. |
| **Design Update Latency** | < 4 hours total | Modifying a mission parameter propagates updates to physical fabrication files in hours instead of days. |
| **MDO Execution Speed** | < 60 minutes | Multidisciplinary design optimization and aero/structural checks run without manual re-meshing. |

---

**Ecosystem Research Facilities**

* **Digital Enterprise Collaboratory (DEC):** Provides high-performance desktop computation and software environments to test industry-standard tools, compute aerodynamic models, and host collaborative training sessions.
* **DTC Model Shop:** Serves as the physical head of the digital thread, utilizing additive manufacturing, optical metrology, and physical test fixtures to validate digital twin predictions.

---

**Planned Digital Case Study (DCS) Reports**

* **DCS-01: MBSE to Parametric CAD Interoperability:** Evaluates data transfer friction when mapping SysML system requirements directly to automated CAD geometry inputs.
* **DCS-02: Physics Middleware Sizing Optimization:** Benchmarks Python-driven aerodynamic sizing models against manual CFD and trade studies.
* **DCS-03: Metrology Twin Validation:** Compares 3D optical scan data of LW-PLA printed wing assemblies against digital CAD envelopes to verify physical accuracy within ±1.5 mm.
